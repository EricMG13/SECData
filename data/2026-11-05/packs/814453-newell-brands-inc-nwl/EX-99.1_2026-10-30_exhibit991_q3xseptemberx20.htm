Newell Brands Announces Third Quarter 2026 Results
Top-Line Growth Accelerates Sequentially, Delivering Second Consecutive Quarter of Sales Growth
Raises Full-Year Sales Outlook


ATLANTA, GA – October 30, 2026 – Newell Brands (NASDAQ: NWL) today announced its third quarter 2026 financial results.

Chris Peterson, Newell Brands President and Chief Executive Officer, said, "Top-line growth accelerated sequentially as Newell Brands delivered its second consecutive quarter of year-over-year growth in sales. Growth during the quarter was broad-based, with both the U.S. and International delivering growth along with four of our six businesses and seven of our top 10 brands. Our investment in consumer understanding, brand building, innovation, and marketing continues to expand distribution and drive growth. Based on these solid results and the dramatic improvements we have made across the key capabilities required to win in our industry, we are raising our full year sales outlook and now expect to deliver positive sales growth in both the fourth quarter and full year 2026."

Mark Erceg, Newell Brands Chief Financial Officer, said, "Stronger sales and world-class productivity efforts allowed third quarter normalized gross and operating margins to expand significantly versus last year and normalized earnings per share to come in at the high end of our guidance range despite inflation that exceeded our expectations. Higher projected sales than previously anticipated and additional cost containment efforts give us the confidence to maintain the midpoints of our normalized operating margin and normalized earnings per share outlooks. Deleveraging remains a top priority and, consistent with that, we anticipate reducing total debt and factoring by a couple hundred million dollars this year."


Third Quarter 2026 Highlights
–Net sales were $1.9 billion, an increase of 3.7% compared with the prior year period. Core sales grew 3.3% compared with the prior year period.
–Gross margin increased to 34.7% compared with 34.1% in the prior year period. Normalized gross margin increased to 35.2% compared with 34.5% in the prior year period.
–Operating margin decreased to 1.4% compared with 6.6% in the prior year period. Normalized operating margin increased to 9.8% compared with 8.9% in the prior year period.
–Net loss was $81 million compared with net income of $21 million in the prior year period. Normalized net income was $86 million compared with $70 million in the prior year period.
–Diluted loss per share was $0.19 compared with diluted EPS of $0.05 in the prior year period. Normalized diluted EPS was $0.20 compared with $0.17 in the prior year period.
–Normalized EBITDA increased to $248 million compared with $225 million in the prior year period.
–Raised the full-year 2026 outlook for net sales and core sales, maintained the midpoints of normalized operating margin and normalized EPS and revised the operating cash flow outlook.

Third Quarter 2026 Operating Results

Net sales were $1.9 billion, an increase of 3.7% compared with the prior year period, reflecting core sales growth of 3.3% and favorable foreign exchange.

Gross margin was 34.7% compared with 34.1% in the prior year period, with the positive impact from higher net sales and gross productivity more than offsetting headwinds from inflation. Normalized gross margin was 35.2% compared with 34.5% in the prior year period.







Operating income was $27 million compared with $119 million in the prior year period. Operating margin was 1.4% compared with 6.6% in the prior year period. The current period included a non-cash impairment charge of $101 million related to an indefinite-lived tradename. Normalized operating income was $184 million, or 9.8% of sales, compared with $162 million, or 8.9% of sales, in the prior year period. The year-over-year increase in normalized results primarily reflected higher net sales and improved gross margin.

Net interest expense was $86 million compared with $83 million in the prior year period.

Income tax benefit was $11 million compared with an income tax provision of $21 million in the prior year period. The normalized income tax provision was $9 million compared with $6 million in the prior year period.

Net loss was $81 million compared with net income of $21 million in the prior year period. Normalized net income was $86 million compared with $70 million in the prior year period. Normalized EBITDA was $248 million compared with $225 million in the prior year period.

Diluted loss per share was $0.19 compared with diluted EPS of $0.05 in the prior year period. Normalized diluted EPS was $0.20 compared with $0.17 in the prior year period.

An explanation of non-GAAP measures disclosed in this release and a reconciliation of these non-GAAP results to comparable GAAP measures, if available, are included in the tables attached to this release.

Balance Sheet and Cash Flow

Year-to-date operating cash flow was $93 million compared with $103 million in the prior year period.

At the end of the third quarter of 2026, Newell Brands had debt outstanding of $4.8 billion and cash and cash equivalents of $249 million, compared with $4.8 billion and $229 million, respectively, at the end of the third quarter of 2025.

During the third quarter of 2026, the Company entered into a new $800 million asset-based revolving credit facility, replacing the existing secured revolving credit facility and extending the facility's general maturity to 2031. The Company also issued $600 million of 6.250% senior notes due 2031 and used the proceeds to redeem its 6.375% senior notes due 2027 and repay a portion of the borrowings under the new facility.

Third Quarter 2026 Operating Segment Results

The Home & Commercial Solutions segment generated net sales of $1.01 billion compared with $942 million in the prior year period, reflecting core sales growth of 6.0%, as well as the impact of favorable foreign exchange. Core sales increased in the Kitchen, Home Fragrance and Commercial businesses. Operating loss was $37 million, or negative 3.7% of sales, compared with operating income of $40 million, or 4.2% of sales, in the prior year period. The current period included a non-cash impairment charge of $101 million related to an indefinite-lived tradename. Normalized operating income was $86 million, or 8.5% of sales, compared with $64 million, or 6.8% of sales, in the prior year period.

The Learning & Development segment generated net sales of $692 million compared with $681 million in the prior year period, reflecting core sales growth of 2.3%, as well as the impact of unfavorable foreign exchange. Core sales grew in the Writing business while the Baby business was roughly flat. Operating income was $137 million, or 19.8% of sales, compared with $124 million, or 18.2% of sales, in the prior year period. Normalized operating income was $157 million, or 22.7% of sales, compared with $130 million, or 19.1% of sales, in the prior year period.

Higher sales expectations and additional cost containment are offsetting additional inflation, enabling the Company to maintain the midpoints of its full-year normalized operating margin and normalized EPS outlooks.

The Company revised its full-year 2026 operating cash flow outlook primarily due to two one-time non-operating items, the first of which relates to the previously announced termination of its non-qualified defined benefit plans. This transaction is expected to generate a net cash benefit of approximately $60 million, but the benefit payments are classified as operating cash outflows while the associated proceeds are classified as investing cash inflows. The accounting treatment lowers expected operating cash flows by approximately $85 million, including related cash taxes. The second item reflects lower than previously anticipated proceeds from receivables factoring as the Company reduces its reliance on these programs following its upsized bond refinancing. Non-GAAP Financial Measures

This release and the accompanying remarks contain non-GAAP financial measures within the meaning of Regulation G promulgated by the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (the "SEC") and includes a reconciliation of non-GAAP financial measures to the most directly comparable financial measures calculated in accordance with GAAP.

The Company uses certain non-GAAP financial measures that are included in this press release, the additional financial information and accompanying remarks both to explain its results to stockholders and the investment community and in the internal evaluation and management of its businesses. The Company’s management believes that these non-GAAP financial measures and the information they provide are useful to investors since these measures (a) permit investors to view the Company’s performance and liquidity using the same tools that management uses to evaluate the Company’s past performance, reportable segments, prospects for future performance and liquidity, and (b) determine certain elements of management incentive compensation.

The Company’s management believes that core sales provides a more complete understanding of underlying sales trends by providing sales on a consistent basis as it excludes the impacts of acquisitions, divestitures, retail store openings and closings, certain market and category exits, changes in foreign exchange and customer returns due to a product recall from year-over-year comparisons. The effect of changes in foreign exchange on reported sales is calculated by applying the prior year average monthly exchange rates to the current year local currency sales amounts (excluding acquisitions and divestitures), with the difference between the current year reported sales and constant currency sales presented as the foreign exchange impact increase or decrease in core sales. The Company’s management believes that “normalized” gross margin, "normalized" overheads, “normalized” operating income, “normalized” operating margin, "normalized EBITDA", “normalized” net income, “normalized” diluted earnings per share and “normalized” income tax benefit or expense, which exclude restructuring and restructuring-related expenses; impairment charges; amortization of acquisition-related intangible assets; divestiture costs; costs related to acquisitions, integration and financing of acquired businesses; hyperinflationary adjustments and one-time and other events such as expenses related to certain legal proceedings, costs related to the extinguishment of debt; certain tax benefits and charges; pension settlement charges; costs related to a product recall; certain facility fire related costs; write-off of assets not placed into service and certain other items, are useful because they provide investors with a meaningful perspective on the current underlying performance of the Company’s core ongoing operations and liquidity. “Normalized EBITDA” is an ongoing liquidity measure (that excludes non-cash items) and is calculated as normalized earnings before interest, tax, depreciation, amortization and stock-based compensation expense.

The Company uses a "with" and "without" approach to calculate normalized income tax expense or benefit. At an interim period, the Company determines the year to date tax effect of the pretax items excluded from normalized results by allocating the difference between the calculated GAAP and calculated normalized tax expense or benefit.

The Company defines "net debt" as short-term debt, current portion of long-term debt and long-term debt less cash and cash equivalents.
