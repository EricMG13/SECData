2026 Guidance6,7

Early Q4 trading has been in line with expectations on an underlying basis, while sports results have been unfavorable in both the US and International. Full year guidance is updated to include the impact of sports results and the previously disclosed impact of the Brazil closure, partially offset by mitigating cost actions. This results in a reduction in full year Group revenue midpoint guidance of $300m to $17.61bn, and a reduction in full year adjusted EBITDA guidance of $160m to $2.495bn.

Dan Taylor, CEO, commented:

“It is a privilege to take on the role of Flutter CEO. I know this business and the industry well, and I have a clear view of our strengths, where we need to improve, and where the biggest opportunities lie.

Online betting and gaming is still a relatively young industry, with significant room to grow as markets develop and customer behavior evolves. Flutter enters its next phase from a position of strength. We have leading businesses in many of the world’s most attractive markets, great brands and scale advantages that few others can match. Our job now is to make more of those advantages.

Our Q3 performance gives me confidence that we are moving in the right direction. In the US, our sportsbook improvement plan is delivering early but tangible progress in customer engagement and performance. Our International business continues to benefit from strong underlying momentum and the impact of our cost transformation programs.

Looking ahead, our priorities are clear: build on our US leadership, capture the prediction markets opportunity, unlock more value from our International portfolio and simplify the way we operate. Delivering against those priorities will require faster execution, tough decisions and disciplined investment. I am confident our actions will make Flutter a stronger business in the long-term.”

To our shareholders,

This is one of my first opportunities as CEO to set out my initial thoughts and priorities. Having worked at Flutter and in the industry for many years, I know the strengths of our business well, and also the improvements required to deliver our next phase of growth.

Flutter is the largest online gambling business with number one positions in many of the world’s most attractive betting and gaming markets. We have fantastic brands, deep sector expertise and significant scale, but we are not getting enough from these advantages. We need to simplify how we operate and move faster, removing costs so that we can invest more behind the right opportunities with the strongest long-term returns.

Our Q3 performance shows that the actions we have already taken are the right ones. Underlying performance in the US was ahead of expectations, while in International we are building on our transformation program and delivering market share gains in our core markets. Sports results went against us in the quarter, however, which is reflected in our updated 2026 guidance.

Against that backdrop I have four immediate operational priorities: building on our US leadership position; capturing the prediction markets opportunity; driving more value from our International portfolio; and simplifying our operating model. Delivering on these priorities will require clear leadership and tough decisions, while remaining disciplined in our investments, focused on improving our cashflow conversion and deleveraging the balance sheet.

Build on our US leadership position

I strongly believe we have the best assets to capture the significant opportunity in the increasingly competitive North American market. However, we must reaffirm our US leadership position by operating at a faster pace, delivering relentless product innovation, and investing where we see the opportunity to deliver long-term value.

We took decisive action earlier in the year to improve our value proposition and increase our share of wallet, including the launch of our FanDuel Rewards program and accelerating our product roadmap. These actions are delivering encouraging returns; our sportsbook performance was ahead of expectations and we took market share.

Customers are responding well to these changes; generosity perception increased seven percentage points8, while player frequency rose 4%, and bet count grew 15%9. Sportsbook revenue increased 4%, reflecting promotional spend increasing to market levels, with strong AMP and handle growth of 9% and 13%, respectively. This good performance provides a solid base for us to build on. We must continue what we are doing well, while ensuring our customer proposition remains at competitive levels. I am confident we are making the right decisions to defend our leadership position and deliver FanDuel’s long-term success.

We continue to see a limited impact from prediction markets on the betting activity of our existing customer base, which I believe, and our consumer research affirms, reflects the superior proposition offered by sportsbooks. We are, however, beginning to see an impact on our overall media costs as prediction market operators increase marketing spend. The duration of this trend will depend in part on how the regulatory landscape develops, but at this early stage, we expect our marketing intensity10 to continue in 2027 at levels similar to this year.

Over the last four years, we have transformed FanDuel Casino from a distant third place, into the market-leader1 by delivering best-in-class content and rewards to our customers. We must ensure we maintain our laser-focus on our customer proposition, delivering market-leading content. Player engagement in Q3 was strong with AMP growth of 24%. Revenue growth moderated to 8% primarily driven by competitive dynamics, and market moderation as the US iGaming states move through the maturity curve. Looking forward, the potential for new states to regulate iGaming provides very significant upside, as evidenced by our very strong launch in Alberta in July.

Capturing the prediction markets opportunity

Prediction markets represent a significant incremental growth area. Our priority is to build a strong position now, investing with discipline and maintaining flexibility to adapt quickly as regulation develops. We believe the actions we have taken in recent months mean we are much better positioned to deliver in three core areas:

First, we must deliver a competitive product. The nationwide launch of our FanDuel One App and the transition of all sports contracts to Crypto.com, means we believe we now have a much more competitive sports offering that customers like and are increasingly engaging with. We have more work to do, and we have a clear plan to further strengthen our customer proposition.

Second, we must use the strength of the FanDuel brand to acquire customers and build a meaningful database in non-sportsbook states. Initial trading volumes are encouraging with total October volume growth almost tripling when compared with July. The customers we are acquiring look like typical sportsbook customers, demonstrating similar characteristics, but with lower costs per acquisition. We are investing at a level that we believe will position us well for the long term, balancing the need to operate at scale in targeted states, against the regulatory uncertainty in this space.

Third, we can use our leading expertise in pricing sports events, and in particular complex correlated events to drive market-making growth. We are making excellent progress, we are on track to deliver our guidance for 2026, and going forward we believe that this growth will help fund our prediction market customer acquisition spend.

Unlock more value from our International portfolio

We have built a portfolio of regional powerhouses with number one positions in many of the world’s major regulated markets. Our scale allows us to invest in better products and customer experiences, while we believe our diversified business delivers resilient growth when individual markets face regulatory or economic change. My focus is on consistent execution, sustained growth and further market share gains.

We made good strategic progress during the quarter, strengthening our market-leading positions in Italy, UKI and CEE. Successful integrations in Italy (Snai), UKI (Sky Bet) and PokerStars are unlocking meaningful revenue upside, with cost benefits expected to be in excess of our $300m target.

In Italy, Sisal delivered another very strong performance supported by a revamped casino experience and an expanded MyCombo product. Snai momentum also gathered pace with the product and generosity upgrades unlocked by the platform migration driving strong AMP growth. Our overall Italian online market share in Q3 grew by 2ppt year-over-year to 32%11 making us the clear number one.

Within UKI a strong performance across our portfolio of leading brands saw us take market share. Record customer engagement during the FIFA World Cup helped deliver good sportsbook handle growth while strong cross-sell helped drive double-digit iGaming revenue growth.

We also recently announced the significant restructuring of our UKI business. We believe this right-sizes the UKI business going forward and positions us well to take market share in the post UK tax increase environment.

While momentum in these regions was strong, our overall International revenue growth was flat year-over-year, as sports results went against us, and the impact of what has become an increasingly challenging trading environment in Brazil played through into our financial performance. Adjusted EBITDA declined by 14% primarily due to the increase in UK gaming taxes.

We are very disappointed with the Government Provisional Measure banning online sports betting and iGaming in Brazil. In response, we have acted decisively; our operations are currently closed, and we have paused all discretionary and uncommitted spend. We have also restructured our business to focus on Betnacional as our sole brand in Brazil, closing our Betfair operations. The previously disclosed challenging trading environment together with the ban and the uncertain regulatory environment, resulted in a non-cash impairment of our assets in Brazil. With the right regulation, Brazil still has the opportunity to be an attractive market for us, however, we will remain disciplined in our deployment of capital as the regulatory environment continues to evolve.

 

4

Despite the regulatory challenges we have faced over the last 12 months, I feel very confident that our International portfolio is now well set to leverage the transformations and cost efficiencies we have delivered, continue to take market share, and deliver against our target of 5-10% annual growth.

Simplifying our operating model to get more value from our scale

The next phase for Flutter will be more streamlined to maximize the benefits of scale and ensure we do non-customer facing work once. That should help us move faster, reduce costs, and improve cash generation, allowing us to invest organically in the areas that drive the highest returns while also deleveraging the balance sheet.

In addition to our existing International cost transformation and UK gaming tax mitigation programs, we expect to deliver an additional $500 million in gross operating cost and capital expenditure savings by 2029. By using AI, removing layers and duplication, and simplifying our technology infrastructure, we can make decisions faster, and direct greater investment towards our customers. In the US, these efficiencies will create more room to invest behind the next stage of growth. Internationally, we expect these benefits to support our previously projected long-term 5–10% annual revenue growth, while protecting adjusted EBITDA margins in more mature markets and funding investment in growth areas.

Final thoughts and outlook

Looking ahead, I am encouraged by the momentum we are building. My focus is on turning our progress in the US into continued delivery, and ensuring we are making the right choices to secure FanDuel’s long-term success. In International, consistent execution and further cost efficiencies should deliver continued profit growth.

Flutter has an exceptional set of assets and opportunities. Realizing their full potential will require hard choices, faster decisions and strong execution. I know confidence is earned through delivery and that is what I am focused on. I look forward to sharing more on our strategy, building on the priorities I have set out today, alongside our Q4 results in February.

 

Dan Taylor
Flutter CEO

US

Revenue grew 6% year-over-year with sportsbook revenue growth of 4% and iGaming revenue growth of 8%. Other revenue grew 20%, primarily driven by our market-making activity in the quarter. AMPs grew 12% year-over-year to 3.9m (sportsbook AMPs +9%, iGaming AMPs +24%, other AMPs +19%).

Sportsbook revenue growth of 4% year-over-year driven by a strong handle performance with 13% year-over-year growth, well ahead of expectations. This was despite a headwind from sporting calendar changes year-over-year with one week less of NFL and CFB. This performance was driven by a strengthened customer proposition which included the expansion of our rewards program to all sportsbook customers and Betprotect+ expanded to NFL. New product features included improved social features such as bet-sharing, and greater personalization with our Re-Use+ and Bet it Again markets.

These product improvements ensured excellent retention of players reactivated and acquired during the World Cup, with an all-time high number of customers entering the football season. We delivered growth in player frequency (+4%) and bet count (+15%) which ensured that handle growth fully offset a 60bps decline in net revenue margin to 6.8%. Net revenue margin included:

 

  •  

No change in structural revenue margin at 12.9% reflecting a higher proportion of MLB, which typically has a lower parlay penetration, during the quarter

 

  •  

No change in the impact of sports results year-over-year (Q3 2026: 10bps unfavorable, Q3 2025: 10bps unfavorable). The in-quarter net revenue impact in Q3 2026 was approximately $38m unfavorable (Q3 2025: $45m unfavorable)

A 60bps increase in promotional spend to 6.0% of handle, in-line with expectations, and reflecting our increased return of value to customers, the impact of new state launches, and investment in the FIFA World Cup. In iGaming, exclusive new content including the launch of Huff ‘n Even More Puff Hard Hat, and content featuring Vegas Matt and Lady Luck helped deliver an increase in player frequency of 12%. These new products and features, together with our very successful launch in Alberta in July where we delivered 23% GGR share in the quarter, drove AMP growth of 24%. Revenue growth of 8% reflected a strong comparative period, which featured the launch of some of our most successful products, and the current year competitive dynamics and market moderation outlined in the shareholder letter.

We are increasingly well-placed to capture the prediction markets opportunity and expect investment in this area to continue in 2027 at similar or higher levels given the early momentum we are delivering.

General and administrative costs were 6% higher year-over-year, reflecting investment in regulatory affairs and prediction markets, partially offset by the benefit of cost savings implemented in Q2.
