Encore Capital Group Announces Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

•Favorable purchasing conditions continue in U.S. market
•Global portfolio purchases of $390 million, including $310 million in U.S.
•Global collections up 14% to record $754 million
•Earnings per share up 76% to $5.57

SAN DIEGO, November 4, 2026 -- Encore Capital Group, Inc. (NASDAQ: ECPG), an international specialty finance company, today reported consolidated financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
“Encore delivered an exceptional third quarter, both financially and operationally, highlighted by strong portfolio purchasing and record global collections,” said Ashish Masih, President and Chief Executive Officer. “Third quarter global portfolio purchases were $390 million and global collections were a record $754 million, up 14% compared to a year ago. This collections performance helped drive GAAP net income in the third quarter of $124 million or $5.57 per share, up 76% compared to $3.17 per share a year ago.”

“MCM, our U.S. business, continues to set the pace. The U.S. market remains highly attractive for us, supported by continued robust portfolio supply, and we are capitalizing on that opportunity with discipline and conviction. MCM purchased $310 million of portfolios in the third quarter and delivered record collections of $589 million, up 17% compared to the third quarter a year ago. This exceptional collections performance is the result of strong execution and continued significant portfolio purchasing as well as the deployment of new technologies, enhanced digital capabilities and continued operational innovation.”
“In Europe, our Cabot business delivered a solid third quarter. Portfolio purchases were $80 million while collections of $164 million were up 2% compared to the third quarter last year.”

“Given the strength and momentum of our business, we are raising our full-year 2026 guidance. We now anticipate global portfolio purchases to be in a range between $1.50 billion and $1.55 billion, and global collections to be in a range between $2.90 billion and $2.95 billion, reflecting year-over-year growth of 12-14%. Additionally, we now expect our EPS in 2026 to be in a range between $15.50 and $16.00 per share, an increase of over 40% compared to 2025. As always, we remain committed to the critical role we play in the consumer credit ecosystem and to helping consumers restore their financial health,” said Masih.

In the third quarter, the company repurchased approximately $27 million of its shares of common stock, bringing total repurchases through the first nine months of 2026 to approximately $73 million.

About Encore Capital Group, Inc.

Encore Capital Group is an international specialty finance company that provides debt recovery solutions and other related services for consumers across a broad range of financial assets. Through its subsidiaries around the globe, Encore purchases portfolios of consumer receivables from major banks, credit unions, and utility providers.
Encore partners with individuals as they repay their debt obligations, helping them on the road to financial recovery and ultimately improving their economic well-being. Encore is the first and only company of its kind to operate with a Consumer Bill of Rights that provides industry-leading commitments to consumers. Headquartered in San Diego, Encore is a publicly traded NASDAQ Global Select company (ticker symbol: ECPG) and a component stock of the Russell 2000, the S&P Small Cap 600 and the Wilshire 4500. More information about the company can be found at http://www.encorecapital.com.
