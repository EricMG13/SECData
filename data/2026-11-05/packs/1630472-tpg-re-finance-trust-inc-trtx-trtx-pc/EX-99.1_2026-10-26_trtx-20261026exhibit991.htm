TPG RE Finance Trust, Inc. Reports Operating Results for the Quarter Ended September 30, 2026
October 26, 2026
NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--TPG RE Finance Trust, Inc. (NYSE: TRTX) (“TRTX” or the “Company”) reported its operating results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Regarding third quarter results, Doug Bouquard, Chief Executive Officer of TRTX, said: “Our third quarter results demonstrate TRTX’s continued successful execution of the business plan we communicated several years ago. During the third quarter, we added $773 million of loan commitments, grew net earning assets by $600 million, and repurchased 1.2 million shares of common stock. Building on this momentum, our Board approved a new $50 million share repurchase program, doubling our prior authorization and underscoring our conviction in the value of TRTX shares and our disciplined approach to capital allocation. We enter the fourth quarter with a well-positioned balance sheet, with a focus on continuing to create long-term value for our shareholders.”
THIRD QUARTER 2026 ACTIVITY
•Recognized GAAP net income attributable to common stockholders of $5.8 million, or $0.08 per common share, based on a diluted weighted average share count of 76.5 million common shares. Book value per common share was $10.85 as of September 30, 2026, compared to $10.95 at June 30, 2026.
•Generated Distributable Earnings and Distributable Earnings Before Realized Losses of $5.1 million, or $0.07 per common share, and $18.1 million, or $0.24 per common share, respectively, based on a diluted weighted average share count of 76.5 million common shares.
•Declared on September 17, 2026 a cash dividend of $0.24 per share of common stock which was paid on October 23, 2026 to common stockholders of record as of September 28, 2026. The Company paid on September 30, 2026 to preferred stockholders of record as of September 18, 2026 a quarterly dividend on its 6.25% Series C Cumulative Redeemable Preferred Stock of $0.3906 per share.
•Repurchased 1,163,018 shares of common stock, at a weighted average price of $8.00 per share, for total consideration (including commissions and related fees) of $9.3 million, which increased book value per common share by $0.04 per common share.
•Originated eight first mortgage loans and acquired one first mortgage loan with aggregate total loan commitments of $772.7 million, an aggregate initial unpaid principal balance of $690.0 million, a weighted average interest rate of Term SOFR plus 3.02%, a weighted average interest rate floor of 2.51% and a weighted average as-is loan-to-value ratio of 70.2%.
•Completed one non-judicial foreclosure resulting in the acquisition of one multifamily property with a fair value of $30.1 million at foreclosure.
•Funded $71.8 million of future funding obligations associated with previously originated and acquired loans.
•Received loan repayments of $119.6 million, including one full loan repayment of $106.0 million, involving the following property types: 88.6% multifamily and 11.4% industrial.
•Weighted average risk rating of the Company’s loan portfolio was 3.0 as of September 30, 2026, unchanged from June 30, 2026.
•Carried at quarter-end an allowance for credit losses of $76.1 million, a decrease of $4.5 million from $80.7 million as of June 30, 2026. The quarter-end allowance of 147 basis points of total loan commitments as of September 30, 2026, represented a decline of 32 basis points from 179 basis points as of June 30, 2026.
•Ended the quarter with $291.6 million of near-term liquidity: $70.8 million of cash-on-hand, of which $18.2 million was available for investment, net of $52.6 million held to satisfy liquidity covenants under the Company’s various financing arrangements; undrawn capacity under secured financing arrangements of $87.3 million; undrawn capacity under asset-specific financing arrangements and secured revolving credit facility of $22.0 million; undrawn capacity of $100.0 million under the Revolver; and collateralized loan obligation reinvestment proceeds held at the trustee of $11.5 million.
•Non-mark-to-market borrowings represented 78.8% of total borrowings at September 30, 2026.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures Reconciliation
Distributable Earnings
Distributable Earnings is a non-GAAP measure, which we define as GAAP net income (loss) attributable to our common stockholders, including realized gains and losses from loan write-offs, loan sales and other loan resolutions (including conversions to real estate owned (“REO”)), regardless of whether such items are included in other comprehensive income or loss, or in GAAP net income (loss), and excluding (i) non-cash stock compensation expense, (ii) depreciation and amortization expense (which only applies to debt investments related to real estate to the extent we foreclose upon the property or properties underlying such debt investments), (iii) unrealized gains (losses) (including credit loss expense (benefit), net), and (iv) certain non-cash or income and expense items.
We believe that Distributable Earnings provides meaningful information to consider in addition to our net income (loss) and cash flow from operating activities determined in accordance with GAAP. We generally must distribute at least 90% of our net taxable income annually, subject to certain adjustments and excluding any net capital gains, for us to continue to qualify as a real estate investment trust for U.S. federal income tax purposes. We believe that one of the primary reasons investors purchase our common stock is to receive our dividends. Because of our investors’ continued focus on our ability to pay dividends, Distributable Earnings is an important measure for us to consider when determining our distribution policy and dividends per common share. Further, Distributable Earnings helps us to evaluate our performance excluding the effects of certain transactions and GAAP adjustments that we believe are not necessarily indicative of our current loan investment and operating activities.
Distributable Earnings excludes the impact of our credit loss provision or reversals of our credit loss provision, but only to the extent that our credit loss provision exceeds any realized credit losses during the applicable reporting period. See Note 2 to our Consolidated Financial Statements included in our Form 10-Q for additional details regarding our accounting policies and estimation of our allowance for credit losses.
Distributable Earnings does not represent net income (loss) or cash generated from operating activities and should not be considered as an alternative to GAAP net income (loss), an indication of our GAAP cash flows from operations, a measure of our liquidity, or an indication of funds available for our cash needs. In addition, our methodology for calculating Distributable Earnings may differ from the methodologies employed by other companies to calculate the same or similar supplemental performance measures, and accordingly, our reported Distributable Earnings may not be comparable to the Distributable Earnings reported by other companies.
