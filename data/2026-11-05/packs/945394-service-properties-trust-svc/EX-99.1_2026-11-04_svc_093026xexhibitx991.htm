Service Properties Trust Announces Third Quarter 2026 Results
Updated Investor Presentation Available on Company Website
Newton, MA (November 4, 2026). Service Properties Trust (Nasdaq: SVC) today announced its financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, which can be found at the Quarterly Results section of SVC’s website at https://www.svcreit.com/investors/financial-information/default.aspx. About Service Properties Trust:
SVC is a real estate investment trust with $9.6 billion invested in two asset categories: service-focused retail net lease properties and hotels. As of September 30, 2026, SVC owned 740 service-focused retail net lease properties with over 13.5 million square feet throughout the United States and 87 hotels with approximately 20,000 guest rooms throughout the United States, including Puerto Rico, and Canada. SVC is managed by The RMR Group (Nasdaq: RMR), a leading U.S. alternative asset management company with approximately $37 billion in assets under management as of September 30, 2026, and 40 years of institutional experience in buying, selling, financing and operating commercial real estate. SVC is headquartered in Newton, MA. For more information, visit www.svcreit.com.
