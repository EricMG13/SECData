Allison Announces Third Quarter 2026 Results

 

  •  

Raising revenue, earnings and cash flow guidance for the second consecutive quarter

 

  •  

Net Sales of $1,534 million, up 121% year over year, including the addition of the Allison Off-Highway business unit acquired on January 1, 2026

 

  •  

Record quarterly net sales of $881 million for the Allison Transmission business unit, up 27% year over year

 

  •  

Net Income of $191 million, 12.5% of Net Sales

 

  •  

Diluted EPS of $2.30, Adjusted Diluted EPS of $2.90, up 59% year over year

 

  •  

Adjusted EBITDA of $400 million, up 56% year over year, 26.1% of Net Sales

INDIANAPOLIS, November 2, 2026 – Allison Transmission Holdings Inc. (NYSE: ALSN), today reported third quarter net sales of $1,534 million with an adjusted EBITDA margin of 26.1 percent and net cash provided by operating activities of $343 million and raised full year 2026 guidance.

David S. Graziosi, Chair, President and Chief Executive Officer of Allison commented, “Allison’s third quarter results reflect continued strength across the majority of our end markets, supported by disciplined execution that maximized the benefits of higher volumes. Within the Allison Transmission business unit, demand accelerated for medium-duty and Class 8 vocational trucks, driving record quarterly revenue of $462 million in the North America On-Highway end market. Within the Allison Off-Highway business unit, momentum in the Mining end market remained robust, while the Construction & Material Handling end market delivered year over year growth across all regions, exceeding our expectations entering 2026. As anticipated, third quarter performance reflects customary seasonality for the business unit, including the impact of European holidays, and represents the softest quarter of the year. Conversely, demand within the Industrial and Agriculture end markets remained subdued, as ongoing geopolitical developments continue to temper customer purchasing activity.”

Graziosi continued, “Favorable end markets demand conditions and operational execution drove strong cash generation in the third quarter, resulting in adjusted free cash flow of $278 million, up 51 percent year over year. Year to date, this cash generation has supported the repayment of over $400 million of debt, advancing the Company toward our leverage target. In addition to debt reduction, Allison remains committed to returning capital to shareholders through our quarterly dividend and share repurchase program. During the third quarter, Allison paid $23 million for its quarterly dividend and repurchased $57 million of common stock.”

Third quarter results include segment reporting for Allison Transmission, the Company’s legacy business, excluding certain costs now accounted for within the Allison Central Group, and Allison Off-Highway, the business acquired from Dana Incorporated on January 1, 2026. The Allison Central Group is a centralized cost center which includes certain functional costs that support the Company’s global operations.

Allison Consolidated Third Quarter Financial Results

Net sales for the quarter were $1,534 million, including the addition of $653 million in net sales for the Allison Off-Highway business unit.

Gross profit for the quarter was $505 million, an increase of $175 million from $330 million for the same period in 2025. The increase was principally driven by the addition of the Allison Off-Highway business unit and a $77 million increase in the Allison Transmission business unit. Gross margin for the quarter was 33 percent.

Selling, general and administrative expenses for the quarter were $142 million, an increase of $59 million from $83 million for the same period in 2025. The increase was principally driven by the addition of the Allison Off-Highway business unit. Selling general and administrative expenses for the third quarter include $4 million of one-time acquisition-related expenses.

Engineering – research and development expenses for the quarter were $59 million, an increase of $16 million from $43 million for the same period in 2025. The increase was principally driven by the addition of the Allison Off-Highway business unit.

Net income for the quarter was $191 million, an increase of $54 million from $137 million for the same period in 2025. The increase was principally driven by increased gross profit from the addition of the Allison Off-Highway business unit, higher gross profit in the Allison Transmission business unit and lower acquisition-related expenses. The year over year increase in net income was partially offset by higher interest expense, net, a $17 million charge attributable to optimization activities within the Allison Off-Highway business unit and higher income tax expense.

Diluted EPS for the third quarter was $2.30, a year over year increase of 41 percent.

Excluding the effect of certain non-cash, non-recurring, infrequent or unusual items, including the costs associated with the acquisition of the Allison Off-Highway business unit, adjusted net income, a non-GAAP financial measure, was $241 million for the third quarter and adjusted diluted EPS was $2.90, a year over year increase of 59 percent.

Adjusted EBITDA, a non-GAAP financial measure, was $400 million for the third quarter, an increase of $144 million from $256 million for the same period in 2025. Adjusted EBITDA margin for the quarter was 26.1 percent.

Net cash provided by operating activities for the quarter was $343 million, a year over year increase of 50 percent. Adjusted free cash flow, a non-GAAP financial measure, for the quarter was $278 million, a year over year increase of 51 percent.

Allison ended the third quarter with nearly $500 million of cash and cash equivalents and $996 million of available borrowing capacity under its revolving credit facility. Allison ended the third quarter with total debt of $4,012 million and net debt of $3,515 million.

During the third quarter, Allison paid a quarterly dividend of $0.29 per share and repurchased $57 million of its common stock, with $1,068 million of authorization remaining under its stock repurchase program.

Allison Transmission Third Quarter Financial Highlights

Net sales for the quarter increased 27 percent from the same period in 2025, leading to record quarterly net sales of $881 million.

Gross profit for the quarter was $407 million, an increase of $77 million from $330 million for the same period in 2025. The increase was principally driven by higher volumes for medium-duty and Class 8 vocational trucks and price increases on certain products, partially offset by unfavorable direct material costs and higher manufacturing expense. Gross margin for the third quarter was 46 percent.

Selling, general and administrative expenses for the quarter were $62 million, an increase of $7 million from $55 million for the same period in 2025 when adjusting for allocations of certain selling, general and administrative expenses to the Allison Central Group. The increase was principally driven by higher incentive compensation expense.

Engineering – research and development expenses for the quarter were $45 million, an increase of $2 million from $43 million for the same period in 2025. The increase was principally driven by higher incentive compensation expense, partially offset by reduced product initiatives spending.

Segment operating profit was $300 million, or 34 percent of net sales, for the third quarter. Adjusted EBITDA, a non-GAAP financial measure, was $333 million for the third quarter. Adjusted EBITDA margin for the quarter was 38 percent.

Allison Off-Highway Third Quarter Financial Highlights

Net sales for the quarter were $653 million.

Gross profit for the quarter was $98 million, representing 15 percent of net sales.

Selling, general and administrative expenses for the quarter were $52 million. Engineering – research and development expenses for the quarter were $14 million.

Segment operating profit was $32 million, or 5 percent of net sales, for the third quarter. Adjusted EBITDA, a non-GAAP financial measure, was $83 million for the third quarter. Adjusted EBITDA margin for the quarter was 13 percent.

Full Year 2026 Guidance Update

Given our third quarter results and improved conditions across the majority of our key end markets, we are increasing our full year 2026 guidance provided to the market on August 3, 2026. Allison expects:

 

  •  

Consolidated net sales in the range of $5,975 to $6,075 million

 

  •  

Consolidated net income in the range of $645 to $685 million, subject to the completion of purchase price accounting associated with the acquisition of the Allison Off-Highway business unit

  •  

Net income guidance includes approximately $125 million of one-time, pre-tax expenses associated with the separation, integration and restructuring of the Allison Off-Highway business unit. This includes restructuring charges related to manufacturing footprint optimization in our European operations and approximately $65 million of expenses related to the stepped-up basis in inventory. Including one-time costs, the Allison Off-Highway acquisition is expected to be accretive to net income and diluted EPS in 2026

 

  •  

Consolidated adjusted EBITDA in the range of $1,530 to $1,580 million

 

  •  

Consolidated net cash provided by operating activities in the range of $1,110 to $1,140 million, including approximately $60 million of one-time cash outlays associated with the acquisition of the Allison Off-Highway business unit

 

  •  

Consolidated capital expenditures in the range of $240 to $260 million, including one-time separation and integration capital expenditures of approximately $20 million

 

  •  

Consolidated adjusted free cash flow in the range of $850 to $900 million

About Allison

Allison (NYSE: ALSN) is a global leader in high-performance mobility and work solutions built for the needs of the modern industrial world. Allison operates through two business units: Allison Transmission and Allison Off-Highway Drive & Motion Systems. Headquartered in Indianapolis, Indiana, USA, the Company manufactures solutions which offer industry-leading value propositions across vital sectors such as infrastructure, mining, energy, agriculture, construction, transportation and national security. For over 110 years, Allison has been recognized as a reliable partner of choice, keeping essential industries moving anytime, in over 150 countries around the world. Use of Non-GAAP Financial Measures

This press release contains information about Allison’s financial results and forward-looking estimates of financial results that are not presented in accordance with accounting principles generally accepted in the United States (“GAAP”). Such non-GAAP financial measures are reconciled to their most directly comparable GAAP financial measures at the end of this press release. Non-GAAP financial measures should not be considered in isolation or as a substitute for our reported results prepared in accordance with GAAP and, as calculated, may not be comparable to other similarly titled measures of other companies.

We use adjusted earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization (“EBITDA”) and adjusted EBITDA as a percent of net sales (“adjusted EBITDA margin”) to measure our operating profitability. We believe that adjusted EBITDA and adjusted EBITDA margin provide management, investors and creditors with useful measures of the operational results of our business and increase the period-to-period comparability of our operating profitability. Adjusted EBITDA margin is also used in the calculation of management’s incentive compensation program. The most directly comparable GAAP measure to adjusted EBITDA and adjusted EBITDA margin is net income or segment operating profit (loss) in the case of our segments and net income as a percent of net sales (“net income margin”) or segment operating profit (loss) as a percent of net sales in the case of our segments, respectively. Adjusted EBITDA is calculated as earnings before interest expense, net, income tax expense, amortization of intangible assets, depreciation of property, plant and equipment and other adjustments as defined by the Second Amended and Restated Credit Agreement dated as of March 29, 2019, as amended, governing Allison Transmission, Inc.’s term loans and revolving credit facility. Adjusted EBITDA margin is calculated as adjusted EBITDA divided by net sales.

In addition, we believe adjusted net income, adjusted basic earnings per share attributable to common stockholders (“adjusted basic EPS”) and adjusted diluted earnings per share attributable to common stockholders (“adjusted diluted EPS”) provide management, investors and creditors with useful measures of our core business performance and trends and increase the period-to-period comparability of our results of operations. The most directly comparable GAAP measure to adjusted net income, adjusted basic EPS and adjusted diluted EPS is net income, basic earnings per share attributable to common stockholders (“basic EPS”) and diluted earnings per share attributable to common stockholders (“diluted EPS”), respectively. Adjusted net income is calculated as net income excluding the effect of certain non-cash, non-recurring, infrequent or unusual items such as: amortization related to acquired intangible assets, depreciation of the stepped-up basis in property, plant and equipment related to acquired assets, stepped-up basis in acquired inventory, stock-based compensation expense, acquisition-related expenses, impairment charges, other one-off adjustments and the tax effect of the adjustments. Adjusted basic EPS is calculated by dividing adjusted net income by the weighted average shares of common stock outstanding and adjusted diluted EPS is calculated by dividing adjusted net income by the diluted weighted average shares of common stock outstanding.

We use adjusted free cash flow to evaluate the amount of cash generated by our business that, after the capital investment needed to maintain and grow our business and certain mandatory debt service requirements, can be used for repayment of debt, stockholder distributions and strategic opportunities, including investing in our business. We believe that adjusted free cash flow enhances the understanding of the cash flows of our business for management, investors and creditors. Adjusted free cash flow is also used in the calculation of management’s incentive compensation program. The most directly comparable GAAP measure to adjusted free cash flow is net cash provided by operating activities. Adjusted free cash flow is calculated as net cash provided by operating activities after cash used for additions of long-lived assets.
