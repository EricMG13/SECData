ENTEGRIS REPORTS RESULTS FOR THIRD QUARTER OF 2026

•Net sales of $1,006 million.
•GAAP diluted EPS of $1.03.
•Non-GAAP diluted EPS of $1.23.

BILLERICA, Mass., October 29, 2026 - Entegris, Inc. (NASDAQ: ENTG), today reported its financial results for the Company’s third quarter ended September 26, 2026.
Dave Reeder, Entegris’ President and Chief Executive Officer, said: “Entegris is executing well against an attractive long-term opportunity, driven by increasing semiconductor complexity, rising materials intensity, and investment in leading-edge technologies. Our ability to deliver the performance, quality, and reliability our customers require is helping us expand plans of record and secure new design wins, driving strong revenue growth across our unit and capex businesses. That growth, combined with disciplined execution, drove another strong quarter, highlighted by record revenue, continued margin expansion and strong earnings growth, all of which exceeded our guidance range. We also delivered record cash flow, enabling us to accelerate debt repayment and further strengthen our balance sheet. We look forward to discussing the significant opportunities ahead for Entegris and our long-term strategy in more detail at our Capital Markets Day on November 9, 2026.”

Fourth Quarter of 2026 Outlook
For the Company’s guidance for the fourth quarter ending December 31, 2026, the Company expects sales of $1,025 million to $1,055 million. The Company expects GAAP net income to be between $168 million and $181 million and diluted earnings per common share is expected to be between $1.09 and $1.17. On a non-GAAP basis, the Company expects diluted earnings per common share to range from $1.32 to $1.40, reflecting net income on a non-GAAP basis in the range of $203 million to $215 million. The Company also expects Adjusted EBITDA of approximately 30.7% to 31.2% of sales.
Segment Results
The Company currently operates in two segments:
Materials Solutions (MS): MS provides materials-based solutions, such as chemical vapor and atomic layer deposition materials, chemical mechanical planarization slurries and pads, ion implantation specialty gases, formulated etch and clean materials, and other specialty materials that enable our customers to achieve better device performance and faster time to yield, while providing for lower total cost of ownership.
Advanced Purity Solutions (APS): APS offers filtration, purification and contamination-control solutions that improve customers’ yield, device reliability and cost by ensuring the purity of critical liquid chemistries and gases and the cleanliness of wafers and other substrates used throughout semiconductor manufacturing processes, the semiconductor ecosystem and other high-technology industries.

About Entegris
Entegris is a leading supplier of critical advanced materials and process solutions for the semiconductor and other high-technology industries. Entegris has approximately 7,700 employees throughout its global operations and is ISO 9001 certified. It has manufacturing, customer service and/or research facilities in the United States, Canada, China, Germany, Israel, Japan, Malaysia, Singapore, South Korea, and Taiwan. Additional information can be found at www.entegris.com.
